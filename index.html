Short Bio

Qinde Chen is a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), working with Prof. Song Guo. He received his Ph.D. degree in Software Engineering from Sun Yat-sen University in June 2026, supervised by Prof. Huawei Huang, after entering a combined Master's–Ph.D. program in 2021. He received his B.E. degree from Sun Yat-sen University in 2021. His research interests include blockchain systems, distributed systems, cross-shard transaction protocols, and DeFi mechanism design.
