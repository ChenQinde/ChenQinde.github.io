Research

My research sits at the intersection of sharding blockchain systems and DeFi mechanism design: how to scale blockchains by splitting them into shards, and how to design the economic rules so that the people who keep such systems running are paid fairly. Below are plain-language summaries of each paper — what problem it tackles, what we did, and my role.

Lead-Author Work

LiquidityPool: Game-Theoretic Analysis of Stakeholder Revenue in Ranking-Dependent DeFi

ACM The Web Conference (WWW '26), 2026 · CCF-A · ACM DL · Code

In ranking-dependent DeFi protocols a stakeholder's revenue depends on how their fund balance ranks against others, so large players consistently out-earn small ones and many small-volume users are effectively shut out. We propose LiquidityPool, a fund-aggregation protocol that lets stakeholders pool capital to participate collectively, and analyze the resulting competition game-theoretically.

  • Identifies how ranking-dependent rewards disadvantage small-volume stakeholders.
  • Proposes LiquidityPool, a protocol that aggregates funds so small stakeholders can participate.
  • Models the setting as a multi-leader, multi-follower Stackelberg competition.
  • Shows that — unlike classical Bertrand/Cournot competition — aggregation can improve social welfare, because convex revenue functions create economies of scale.

My role: First author — problem formulation, protocol design, and game-theoretic analysis.

Broker2Earn: Towards Maximizing Broker Revenue and System Liquidity for Sharded Blockchains

IEEE INFOCOM '24, 2024 · CCF-A · IEEE Xplore

Sharding splits a blockchain into parallel shards, but cross-shard transactions are slow because they must reach consensus in two shards. Broker accounts can turn cross-shard transactions into fast intra-shard ones — but only if enough users are willing to act as brokers. Broker2Earn is a DeFi-style incentive mechanism that rewards users for collateralizing tokens as brokers.

  • Designs an incentive mechanism where brokers earn revenue by staking (collateralizing) tokens.
  • Formulates the core broker problem as revenue maximization and proves it NP-hard.
  • Solves it with an online approximation algorithm (relax-and-rounding) with a proven approximation ratio.
  • Evaluated on real-world Ethereum transactions, achieving near-optimal broker revenue and system-liquidity usage.

My role: First author — design, optimization formulation, and experiments.

iASTMapper: An Iterative Similarity-Based Abstract Syntax Tree Mapping Algorithm

IEEE/ACM ASE '23, 2023 · CCF-A · IEEE Xplore

To understand how source code changes between two versions, tools match nodes of the program's abstract syntax tree (AST). iASTMapper computes these mappings iteratively using similarity, producing more accurate and stable matches than prior approaches.

  • Key insight: mappings of lower-level nodes (tokens) and higher-level nodes (statements) mutually influence each other — an effect prior tools ignore.
  • Iteratively maps tokens, statements, and inner-statements by similarity, connecting the three processes.
  • Outperforms GumTree, MTDiff, and IJM, evaluated on 210,997 file revisions from ten Java projects.

My role: Corresponding author — algorithm design and evaluation.

Collaborative Work

Aiming Low-Latency Atomicity for Cross-Shard Transactions in a Sharding Blockchain

IEEE ICDCS '26, 207–217, 2026 · CCF-B · DOI

Cross-shard transactions normally wait for consensus in more than one shard, creating substantially longer confirmation latency than intra-shard transactions. This work presents a decentralized online scheduling approach that reduces cross-shard queueing latency while preserving transaction atomicity.

My role: Co-author.

BrokerChain: A Blockchain Sharding Protocol by Exploiting Broker Accounts

IEEE Transactions on Networking, 2025 · CCF-A · IEEE Xplore

BrokerChain is the protocol foundation behind much of our broker-based work. Built for account-based state sharding, it uses fine-grained state partition and account segmentation, and handles cross-shard transactions through broker accounts — balancing transaction workload across shards and reducing the number of cross-shard transactions (avoiding "hot shards").

My role: Co-author — contributed to the broker-account mechanism and analysis.

BlockEmulator: An Emulator Enabling to Test Blockchain Sharding Protocols

IEEE Transactions on Services Computing, 2025 · CCF-A · IEEE Xplore

Testing sharding protocols on real networks is hard and costly. BlockEmulator is an open experimental platform that lets researchers prototype, run, and measure sharding protocols (including cross-shard mechanisms) in a controlled, reproducible environment.

My role: Co-author — contributed to design and experimental evaluation.

ShellFusion: An Answer Generator for Shell Programming Tasks via Knowledge Fusion

IEEE/ACM ICSE '23 (Demonstrations), 2023 · IEEE Xplore

ShellFusion is a web-based tool that helps developers with shell-programming questions by fusing multi-source knowledge — from Q&A posts, Ubuntu Manual Pages, and TLDR tutorials — to generate relevant commands, scripts, and explanations.

My role: Co-author.
